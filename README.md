# My Favorite Movie

## 7 Sundays (2017)

My favorite movie is 7 Sundays, a Filipino family drama movie about four siblings who reunite after learning that their father is sick.

### Why I Like It
- It shows the importance of family.
- It teaches us to forgive one another.
- It reminds us to spend time with our loved ones.
- It shows that family problems can be solved through understanding and communication.

### My Favorite Lesson
The movie taught me that family is important, and we should value every moment we spend with our loved ones because time is precious.

**7 Sundays is my favorite movie because it reminds me that family will always be important in our lives.**
